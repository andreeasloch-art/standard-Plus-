Pricing

Two options – you choose

Either you pay per successful placement or you book the subscription with all-round support. Both without hidden costs. All prices plus statutory VAT.

Model A · One-time payment

If you only need the placement

399 – 1.999 €

per successful placement, due only after the contract is signed. No base fee, no minimum term.

Model B · Subscription

If you want all-round support

€399 + €119/month

one-off start-up fee, then monthly – unlimited placements and support from the search until after the first day at work.

Rule of thumb: if you handle induction, travel and paperwork yourself, the one-off payment suits you well. If you want everything from a single source, choose the subscription.
