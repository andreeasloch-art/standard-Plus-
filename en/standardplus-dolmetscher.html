Interpreter

Everyone speaks their own language

You speak English, the other person hears Romanian – and vice versa. Two devices, one code. A microphone and speaker are enough, no headphones needed.

1 · YOUR LANGUAGE

Nobody sets the target language – it follows from what the other side has chosen.

2 · CODE

Read it out to the other side on the phone or send it by message.

3 · IHR NAME

Only shown to the other person. Not stored.

4 · HOW YOU SPEAK

Via microphone and speaker

You need nothing but your device. While the translation is read out, the microphone pauses briefly – otherwise it would hear its own output and translate it again. So you take turns speaking, just as with an interpreter.

No interpreter server configured. Enter the address in assets/sp-konfig.js, then you can speak here.
Connected.

YOU SAY

Start speaking. What the server understood appears here.

YOU HEAR

As soon as the other side speaks, it appears here – and you hear it in your language.

Where your voice goes: only to the server whose address is configured in this application. No second recipient, no recording, no storage. The audio is discarded after each sentence. Original and translation always appear side by side so that misunderstandings are noticed.
Not a binding translation. For salary, working hours and safety commitments, a human should be involved, or a written confirmation in both languages.
