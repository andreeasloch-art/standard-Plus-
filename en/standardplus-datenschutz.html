3. Categories of data processed

3.1 For applicants

  • Master data: name, address, date of birth, nationality
  • Contact details: email address, phone number
  • Qualification data: degrees, references, recognition decisions, language level, work experience, driving licences and certificates
  • Availability, preferred region and salary expectation
  • Communication data from messages and interview appointments
  • Information on residence and work permits, where legally required

3.2 For companies and agencies

  • Company data, commercial register number, VAT identification number
  • Contact details of authorised contact persons
  • Contract and billing data

3.3 Special categories

We process health-related information only if it is legally required for carrying out the work, such as occupational health fitness certificates. The legal basis is Art. 9(2)(b) GDPR in conjunction with § 26(3) BDSG, or your explicit consent. We do not collect information on religion, trade union membership, ethnic origin or sexual orientation and ask you not to enter it in free-text fields.

5. Recipients

Data is only transferred where necessary:

  • to companies that have requested a profile – only after your explicit approval
  • to processors under a contract pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR, in particular hosting, email delivery and translation service – all located in the EU
  • to authorities, where there is a legal obligation

Data is not passed on to advertising networks, data brokers or social networks.

6. Transfer to third countries

Processing and backups take place exclusively in data centres within the European Union. No data is transferred to third countries. Should this change in future, we will inform you in advance and base the transfer on an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses, including an assessment of the level of protection in the destination country.

8. Automated decisions and profiling

Our system calculates a score for the professional match between a profile and a position. This score is a pre-sorting and serves as a decision aid. No rejection or acceptance takes place solely on the basis of automated processing; every decision is made and taken responsibility for by a human.

You have the right to learn the criteria of the calculation, to state your point of view and to request a review by a person (Art. 22(3) GDPR). The criteria used can be viewed in your account.

10. Server logs

When you visit, technically necessary data is logged: shortened IP address, time, requested resource, amount transferred, status code and browser type. Storage serves operational security and defence against attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). It is deleted after 14 days unless a security incident requires longer retention.

11. Your rights

  • Access to the data stored about you (Art. 15)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • Erasure, unless a retention obligation prevents it (Art. 17)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability in a structured format (Art. 20)
  • Objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • Withdrawal of consent given, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3))

You can exercise these rights directly in the privacy centre or informally at [datenschutz@ihre-domain.de]. We reply within one month.

Irrespective of this, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular at your place of residence or our registered office: [enter the competent state data protection authority].

13. Changes to this policy

We adapt this policy when processing changes. We announce significant changes in your account at least 30 days in advance. Earlier versions are available on request.
