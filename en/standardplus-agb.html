Draft: This text is a framework and does not replace legal advice. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before use – in particular the fee rules towards jobseekers (§§ 296 et seq. SGB III), liability and prohibited clauses under §§ 305 et seq. BGB.

§ 1 Scope

(1) These terms apply to the use of the Standard Plus platform by companies, staffing agencies and jobseekers.

(2) Deviating terms of users do not become part of the contract unless we expressly agree to their validity in writing.

§ 2 Services

(1) Standard Plus provides a platform on which companies and jobseekers find each other. We owe the provision of the platform, not the success of a placement.

(2) Depending on the chosen model, the scope of services includes profile search, professional matching, video interviews with translation, contract handling and coordination of travel.

(3) Professional matching is a decision aid. The selection decision is always made by the company.

§ 3 Registration and account

(1) Use requires an account. Information must be truthful and kept up to date.

(2) Login details must be kept secret. Two-factor authentication is mandatory for company accounts. Any suspected misuse must be reported immediately to [security@ihre-domain.de].

(3) We may suspend accounts if there is reasonable suspicion of misuse, false information or legal violations.

§ 4 Obligations of users

(1) Only data that users are entitled to process may be uploaded. Creating profiles of third parties without their knowledge is prohibited.

(2) Contact details released via the platform may only be used for the purpose of filling the specific position. Passing them on to third parties, advertising and building own databases are prohibited.

(3) Job advertisements must be worded free of discrimination within the meaning of the German General Equal Treatment Act.

(4) Automated scraping of the platform is prohibited.

§ 5 Fees

(1) The amounts shown on the pricing page apply. All prices are subject to statutory VAT.

(2) The fee for an individual placement becomes due when the employment contract between the company and the placed person is concluded.

(3) No fee is charged to jobseekers for the placement.

(4) Invoices are payable within 14 days without deduction.

§ 6 Term and termination

(1) Subscriptions have a minimum term of six months and are then extended indefinitely.

(2) After the minimum term, the subscription can be cancelled at any time with one month's notice. Cancellation is possible in the account and in text form.

(3) The right to terminate for good cause remains unaffected.

§ 7 Replacement

If a placed person leaves within the first three months for reasons for which the company is not responsible, a new placement is made at no additional charge. This requires notification within 14 days of the departure.

§ 8 Data protection

The processing of personal data is governed by the privacy policy. Users who process third-party data via the platform are independently responsible for this and indemnify us against third-party claims arising from a breach of this obligation.

§ 9 Availability

We aim for an average annual availability of 99 per cent. Announced maintenance windows and outages for which we are not responsible are excluded.

§ 10 Liability

(1) We are liable without limitation for intent and gross negligence and for injury to life, body and health.

(2) In the case of simple negligence, we are only liable for the breach of an essential contractual obligation, limited to the foreseeable damage typical for the contract.

(3) We are not liable for the accuracy of information uploaded by users or for an employment relationship coming about.

§ 11 Right of withdrawal for consumers

Consumers have a right of withdrawal of 14 days. Details and the model withdrawal form can be found in the withdrawal policy. Companies have no right of withdrawal.

§ 12 Changes to these terms

We announce changes in text form at least six weeks before they take effect. If you do not object within this period, the changes are deemed accepted. We point out the significance of silence separately in the announcement.

§ 13 Final provisions

(1) The law of the Federal Republic of Germany applies, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods. For consumers, this applies only insofar as mandatory protective provisions of their country of residence are not restricted.

(2) The place of jurisdiction for merchants is [place].

(3) Should any provision be invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions remains unaffected.
