Who sees how much?

The same data, four levels. This is the core of the data protection concept: there is no way to contact details or documents except through mutual approval.

LevelWhoVisibleHidden
Publicnot signed in Occupation, sector, region, experience everything else
Restrictedsigned in, no approval everything professional, languages, career, ratings Surname, contact details, names of former employers, document contents
Full viewmutual approval everything, documents downloadable nothing
Blockedone side has blocked nothingeverything
For sample accounts only: The sample view only works for the fictitious demo profiles. If the parameter is added to a real account, the normal graded visibility still applies – otherwise the protection would be worthless.
