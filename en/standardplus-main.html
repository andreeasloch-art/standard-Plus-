How it works

From profile to contract in four steps

Every step is documented and traceable for both sides.

01

Step 01

Create a profile

Record qualifications, language level and preferences. Credentials are stored encrypted and only shared after explicit approval.

02

Step 02

Receive suggestions

The matching weighs qualification, experience, region and availability. The weighting can be viewed at any time.

03

Step 03

Approve on both sides

Contact details only become visible once both sides agree. No unsolicited contact, no sharing with third parties.

04

Step 04

Interview and contract

Video interview with live translation, followed by digital contract signing and coordination of travel.

Features

All modules work together

From the first suggestion to the first working day – without switching systems.

Matching

Weighs more than 40 criteria and explains every suggestion transparently. No automated rejection without human review (Art. 22 GDPR).

Transparent

Live translation

Real-time translation in 24 EU languages directly in the video interview. Processing on EU servers, recording only with the consent of both sides.

24 languages

Digital contracts

eIDAS signature, audit-proof storage and automatic deletion periods per document type.

eIDAS

Transport and arrival

Travel, accommodation and appointments with authorities coordinated via the platform – with status tracking for everyone involved.

Reports

Figures on durations, reasons for drop-outs and success rates. Export as CSV or PDF, aggregated and without personal reference.

Security centre

Two-factor sign-in, device overview, access log and a reporting channel for vulnerabilities – visible for every account.
